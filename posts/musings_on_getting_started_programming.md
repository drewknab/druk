The earliest code I ever wrote was proceedural.

<!--more-->

My first experience with writing code was BASIC on the [TI-99/4A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TI-99/4A).
It was a second hand computer that my parents bought from my uncle Larry, God rest him, who had himself
bought it brand new ten years prior, when Texas Instruments was wildly scrambling to sell the damn thing in 1983 after getting into a price war with Commodore.

I was somewhere between eight and nine years old and I already had a fascination with computers. Videogames
had something to do with it, of course. Not to mention "Star Trek", "Tron", "Wargames", "The Last Starfighter" among others.
